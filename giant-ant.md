# Giant Ant

*Small beast, unaligned*

Colony insects grown to the size of a small to medium sized dog — segmented, chittering, and single-minded. They rarely 
act alone; when one attacks, watch for the rest of the nest closing in from the sides.

**Armor Class** 13 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 7 (2d6)
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 11 (+0) | 1 (−5) | 9 (−1) | 3 (−4) |

**Senses** darkvision 30 ft., passive Perception 9
**Languages** —
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)

***Standing Grip.*** The ant can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an 
                     ability check.

***Keen Smell.*** The ant has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

***Colony Coordination.*** The ant has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the ant's 
                     allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

**Actions**

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d6) piercing damage.

---

## GM Notes

- Designed as a "worker/soldier" variant, distinct from the Monster Manual's Large CR 1/2 Giant Ant — this one is Small 
  and meant to appear in swarming groups of 4–8 rather than alone.
- *Colony Coordination* is a reskinned Pack Tactics: it only triggers when an ally is within 5 feet of the **target**, 
  not the attacker. That means the ants are dangerous when clustered around one character, but a lone ant separated from 
  its packmates loses the bonus entirely. 
  Will attack any threat attacking ant(s) within 50 feet or 50 feet of any ant responding to such an attack.
  - **Player incentive:** isolate ants one at a time — kill them away from the cluster, use choke points, pick off 
                          strays — rather than letting the whole swarm mob a single party member.
- Consider pairing with a larger "Ant Queen" or "Soldier Ant" (higher CR) as a nest's boss encounter, with these as the rank and file.
- Climb speed + Standing Grip lets them flank from walls/ceilings — good for tunnel or lair encounters.
